Walk with WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, use the thumbstick. Drag the world to rotate the camera; scroll to zoom. Press E to wave.

Voice is optional. “Join voice” asks for microphone access. “Leave voice” stops capture. Voice is shared with others who join voice in your room. Peer connections may reveal network addresses to room participants.
