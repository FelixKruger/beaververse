# Lantern Grove · builder village

Selected: original giant-tree lodges and elevated timber walks, warm lantern light, workbenches, moss paths, a shared fire, and small beaver builders. Ground-level social space plus a reachable canopy workshop. The larger village is a future expansion, not an empty map in this slice.

Visual target: rich woodland depth with readable character silhouettes, deliberate mass and shape, a restrained warm/cool palette and expressive movement. Scott McCloud's Big Triangle is a lens for balancing reality, iconic meaning and nonrepresentational shape; it is not a requirement for a comic look or a universal taste metric. [Author overview](https://scottmccloud.com/2-print/1-uc/index.html).

The liked base style is a chestnut upright beaver with a blue builder vest and belt pouch. **The owner rejected tail orientation/anatomy in the three subsequent references.** Those images are superseded exploration and must not be submitted to Tripo. Five more distinct beaver types are being explored separately with an independent prompt critique and corrected centered lower-back tail attachment. No corrected conversion-ready reference or rigged/optimized mesh is yet confirmed here.

Local dated reference images are preserved under ignored `design/reference-images/2026-11-04/`. They are deliberately separate from public runtime assets and verification screenshots. The generated concept is the target; it is not evidence of achieved visual fidelity, performance or playability.

## Current visual limits
The first world uses licensed modular assets and a temporary unrigged animal. It proves navigation, shared presence and the spatial arrangement; the hero avatar and more bespoke architectural/material detail are still needed to reach the selected concept's richer visual bar. Do not present this prototype as final art quality.
