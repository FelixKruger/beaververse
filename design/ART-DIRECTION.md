# Lantern Grove · builder village

Selected: original giant-tree lodges and elevated timber walks, warm lantern light, workbenches, moss paths, a shared fire, and small beaver builders. Ground-level social space plus a reachable canopy workshop. The larger village is a future expansion, not an empty map in this slice.

Visual target: rich woodland depth with readable character silhouettes, deliberate mass and shape, a restrained warm/cool palette and expressive movement. Scott McCloud's Big Triangle is a lens for balancing reality, iconic meaning and nonrepresentational shape; it is not a requirement for a comic look or a universal taste metric. [Author overview](https://scottmccloud.com/2-print/1-uc/index.html).

The current approved design reference is the revised **Sculpted Maker**: broad animal-like stance, readable sculpted forms, chestnut fur, neutral canvas/sage vest, softened brow/muzzle, and a centered lower-spine tail root with upward-facing paddle. **Canopy Explorer** is secondary. The owner approved the completed revision on 5 November 2026 and requested a bounded Tripo trial; no mesh/rig or 3D quality is yet established. Earlier tail-rejected references are superseded and must not be used for the trial.

The approved reference and manifest are preserved under ignored `design/reference-images/2026-11-05/`; earlier exploration remains under `2026-11-04/`. They are separate from public runtime assets and verification screenshots. The generated concept is the target; it is not evidence of achieved visual fidelity, performance or playability.

## Current visual limits

This remains an interim approximation, not the approved humanoid builder. It proves navigation, shared presence and the spatial arrangement; the hero avatar and more bespoke architectural/material detail are still needed to reach the selected concept's richer visual bar. Do not present this prototype as final art quality.
