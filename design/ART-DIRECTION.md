# Lantern Grove art direction

Build an original woodland settlement with timber workshops, elevated walks, warm lanterns and cool forest depth. Keep a compact, discoverable place with playful details and clear routes on both phones and computers.

Use deliberate shape, expressive movement and readable silhouettes. Keep the arrival view open, frame the climb to the canopy workshop and make destinations visible from the ground. Surface detail should support the composition. The ground workshop uses a licensed KayKit lumbermill; shared collision bounds must match its footprint.

The current Gobkit Beaver is a low-poly animated quadruped with a scarf and paddle-tail greeting. It is an interim approximation of the intended expressive woodland builder. Follow docs/CUSTOM-AVATAR-BRIEF.md for future replacements and ASSET-LICENSES.md for redistribution rules.

Evaluate changes in the actual camera and lantern lighting. Check tail and muzzle visibility, avatar scale beside buildings, walkable paths, keyboard/touch navigation and performance. Screenshots do not prove phone performance or comfortable movement; test the playable world too.

Do not bundle private references, borrowed brand artwork or uncertain-rights models. A coherent original world matters more than matching a particular commercial game.
