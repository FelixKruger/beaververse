# Beaververse design and evolution playbook

Draft 0.1 · 17 November 2026 · [Principles](VISION-AND-PRINCIPLES.md)

## Player promise

Arrive curious. Feel welcome. Find someone or something worth spending time with. Take away a useful idea, a connection, a small creation, or simply a good experience. Return to a place that remembers its shared history without watching everything you do.

The first experience should work before autonomous evolution exists. Build around one inhabited clearing, a reachable workshop and a readable contribution surface. Keep the Lantern Grove / Woodland Lodge direction: warm light, coherent timber, living forest depth, crafted objects and expressive beavers. Preserve the current world while upgrading a few high-impact assets; an unrelated asset collection is not art direction.

## The first visit

1. **Arrival:** guest name, optional remembered local profile, microphone off. Offer exploration before accounts or agent setup. Explain capture briefly at the relevant action, with full details available.
2. **Orientation:** the fire is social; the workshop is for making; the contribution board shows something a member made and a way to help. Clear paths and framing teach movement without a tutorial wall.
3. **A useful encounter:** exchange a tool, a project link, a question or an idea. A future NPC guide should answer from a small approved resource collection, admit uncertainty and never pretend to be a person.
4. **An act of authorship:** place a bounded contribution or suggest a world change. Show who owns it and whether it is a local draft, proposal, preview or accepted shared object.
5. **Takeaway:** voluntarily write or review a small note to take home. An export for another AI is untrusted reference data with provenance, not permission to act.
6. **Departure:** “Was this visit valuable?” Yes / No / Skip. Optional reason and separate world suggestion. No recurring interruption and no punishment for leaving without answering.
7. **Return:** show one understandable accepted change linked to consented contributions. Do not promise a new world every night or manufacture a reason to claim personalization.

## Contribution ladder

| Level | What the participant may do | What remains controlled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visitor | Explore, talk, keep a personal note, leave feedback | No automatic transcript or behavioral history |
| Contributor | Submit a suggestion; make a bounded owned decoration in a designated area | Templates, quota, occupancy and ownership; no arbitrary scripts or imported files |
| Agent companion | Use a short-lived grant chosen by its owner | Separate observe/wave/move/propose scopes; no microphone, raw chat history, account or filesystem access |
| Builder | Propose a versioned change with assets, rationale and tests | Preview before shared application; provenance and protected landmarks |
| Steward | Review shared changes and reports | Logged decisions; explicit release authority; appeal and rollback |

Start with guests and one companion per active owner. A displayed name is not a verified identity. Persistent profiles, trusted roles, public admission and moderation require a separate identity design before broad launch. Local convenience profiles must be labeled as such.

## Evidence to evolution

Consent-bearing signal → bounded triage → design hypothesis → one candidate → isolated tests and preview → independent review → authorized acceptance → versioned release → compare with baseline → retain or restore.

- **Evidence:** keep source, time, world version and allowed purpose. Private takeaway text must not leak into community reports. A copied recommendation is not a new independent vote.
- **Interpretation:** state the problem and uncertainty. “I got lost” can mean camera, signposting, movement, or social uncertainty. Ask or test; do not blindly implement the suggested solution.
- **Proposal:** one player benefit, a small diff, affected places, permissions, rights, cost estimate, measurable success and rollback plan. No requirement to fill a quota of ideas.
- **Verification:** schema, permission, content, performance and regression gates. Compare two independent browsers, keyboard/touch and a fresh visitor. Real phone and cross-network voice checks remain separate.
- **Acceptance:** shared world changes need the designated human decision until a specific low-risk automatic class has been tested and authorized. Expensive models do not bypass this gate.
- **Continuity:** protect active sessions. Apply compatible additions at explicit boundaries; announce disruptive maintenance. Restoring an older snapshot must not silently delete newer unrelated work.

The first bounded loop processes explicit exported feedback and emits a proposal/check receipt. It has no general shell, production credentials, publisher or paid-model authority. The runnable loop's actual abilities and stopping rules belong in its own README, not in an aspirational claim of full autonomy.

## AI roles and cost

- **Ordinary code:** movement, collision, quotas, access checks, retention and budget accounting. Do not pay a model per animation frame.
- **Small/local model:** optional tagging, deduplication, retrieval and a limited guide. It can abstain and cannot make consequential sanctions or releases.
- **Stronger designer:** reads a small permitted evidence bundle, proposes a coherent change and explains the tradeoff.
- **Separate critic/tester:** checks the candidate against the same fixed principles and a baseline. Different models can help diversity, but two agreeing models are not proof.
- **Human steward:** decides contested taste, privacy, authority changes and publication. Builders cannot self-approve governance edits.

Evaluate model choices against a small held-out set: vague feedback, conflicting preferences, prompt-injected notes, beginner mistakes, accessible alternatives and a legitimate no-change case. Measure useful proposals per total cost, not output volume. Do not send private conversation to a free API merely because it has quota.

## Data contract and comfortable participation

Default: no transcript recording, no ambient summarization, no detailed movement-history storage. Temporary live positions are needed for multiplayer; that is separate from recording a history for analytics.

Personal notes stay on the participant's device unless they export them. Browser storage is not encrypted private storage; other users or software with access to that browser/device may read it. Provide deletion and do not store access tokens there.

Future shared summaries require an explicit start action, all-participant participation rules, a visible indicator, late-arrival handling, immediate stop, transcript review and short retention. Export and world-improvement consent are separate. A general entry notice cannot substitute for those controls.

Future hosted access should distinguish participant, moderator, service worker and emergency administrator. Use server-side authorization, encryption in transit/at rest, minimal operational logs and audited exceptional access. A conventional cloud model must process supplied text; neither this nor ordinary server administration permits a truthful “only AI sees it” promise. Specify subprocessors, region, retention, deletion and legal responsibilities before collecting conversation.

## Abuse response without suspicion of everyone

Start with exact constraints: reject unsupported actions; cap messages and creations; isolate rooms; expire grants; revoke on owner departure. Offer personal mute/block and a report path before broad public access. Keep reports private to authorized review.

Distinguish spam/exploit attempts from awkward speech or rough art. Offer a recoverable draft and coaching for design issues. Restrict only the affected capability where practical. Explain consequential restrictions and provide review; do not infer an enduring malicious identity from a model score. Guest re-entry can bypass identity-based bans, so anonymous openness is not an abuse solution.

## Art and asset gate

Check license for the intended distribution, source URL, original hash and modifications. Inspect mesh scale, silhouettes, normals, topology, texture seams, animation and collisions in the real camera. Use LODs and texture budgets, then measure on representative phones. Paid RUTZ assets and conversions stay private until the publication lane clears the intended packaging. Mixamo/Sketchfab access is not automatic permission to redistribute every asset.

## Six review personas

These are test lenses, not fabricated participant research.

| Persona | Task | Failure that blocks acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curious newcomer | Enter, find company, leave without setup | Needs an account/agent/voice or cannot identify the next action |
| Quiet learner | Learn and retain a private takeaway | Pressure to speak, record or publish |
| Phone visitor | Navigate, open feedback, close it and move again | Small targets, offscreen controls, stuck movement or poor measured device performance |
| Beginner builder | Make and undo one safe contribution | Accidental shared destruction or unexplained rejection |
| Agent owner | Pair, verify scopes, revoke | Undisclosed speech/movement, reusable expired grant, cross-room access |
| Skeptical steward | Inspect one proposal and restore a failure | Missing evidence, fabricated approval, secret change or no reliable rollback |

Human playtest: observe without coaching, ask what was valuable, and test whether the intended change is recognized on return before explaining it. Record voluntary return separately from a scheduled second visit. A “Yes” score is one signal, not a reward function to maximize at any cost.
